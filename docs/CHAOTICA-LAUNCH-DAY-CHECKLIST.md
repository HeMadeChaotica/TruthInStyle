# CHAOTICA Launch Day Checklist

Use this only after the new Vercel deployment is marked **READY**.

## Your five-minute launch check

1. Open `https://www.tellnolies.app/` and unlock CHAOTICA normally.
2. Open the Control Panel and use the **Eye of Truth** to choose today.
3. Press the **Crystal Wand** once. It should open **THE.SUMMATION** and begin the Day Capsule render inside the large crystal screen.
4. When the completed image appears, confirm the title/date strip is correct and the image stays fully inside the crystal frame.
5. Press **So Let It Be Done** once to seal that completed day.
6. Open **HOPEWOOD**. The same sealed image should appear in its large crystal screen.
7. In HOPEWOOD, test all three lookup modes:
   - **DATE:** select the sealed date.
   - **WORDS:** type a word from that day's title or record.
   - **FEELING:** select the day's saved mood.
8. Confirm the matching archive thumbnail selects the correct full-size day.

## If the render does not complete

1. Do not press the Crystal Wand repeatedly.
2. Open **Render details** inside THE.SUMMATION.
3. Copy the exact status text and send it to Codex. Do not send passwords, API keys, or environment values.

## First-day habit

1. Fill the day's sections during the day.
2. Use the Eye of Truth to confirm the correct date before rendering.
3. Render once with the Crystal Wand.
4. Review the day in THE.SUMMATION.
5. Seal it with So Let It Be Done only when the day is final.
